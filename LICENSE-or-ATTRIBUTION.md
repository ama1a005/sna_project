## Dataset Attribution & Acknowledgements

This research makes use of datasets provided by the Stanford Network Analysis Platform (SNAP):

1. **Reddit Hyperlink Network**:
   - Kumar, S., Hamilton, W. L., Leskovec, J., & Jurafsky, D. (2018). *Community Interaction and Conflict on the Web*. In Proceedings of the 2018 World Wide Web Conference (WWW '18), pp. 933–943.
   - Available at: https://snap.stanford.edu/data/soc-RedditHyperlinks.html

2. **Reddit Subreddit Embeddings**:
   - 300-dimensional continuous vector representations generated via Word2Vec over subreddit comment co-occurrences.
   - Available at: https://snap.stanford.edu/data/web-RedditEmbeddings.html
